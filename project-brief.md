## Purpose of This Build

This is a **demo landing page** built to show prospective Sower Creative Tech clients
what a Tier 1 Landing Page looks like in practice. It should represent the full scope
and quality of a real Tier 1 delivery — not a shortcut version.

The fictional business (Cascade Clean Co.) was chosen because:
- It's a different industry from Multi-Contracting (shows range)
- House cleaning is a universally relatable local service
- A single-page landing page is a natural fit for this type of business

### Services
1. **Regular Cleaning** — Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly visits to keep your home consistently fresh. We handle dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bathrooms, and kitchen surfaces on every visit.
2. **Deep Cleaning** — An intensive top-to-bottom clean for homes that need extra attention. Includes inside appliances, baseboards, window sills, and all the spots regular cleaning doesn't reach.
3. **Move-In / Move-Out** — Leave your old home spotless or arrive to a fresh start. We handle the full clean so you can focus on the move itself.
4. **Vacation Rental Turnover** — Fast, reliable cleanings between guests with linen service coordination. We help your Airbnb or VRBO stay guest-ready year-round.

### Why Choose Us
1. **Vetted & Insured Team** — Every cleaner on our team is background-checked, insured, and trained to our standard. Your home and belongings are always protected.
2. **Same Team Every Visit** — We assign you the same cleaners so they learn your home and your preferences. No explaining things twice.
3. **Satisfaction Guaranteed** — If something isn't right, tell us within 24 hours and we'll return to fix it. No questions, no hassle.
4. **Locally Owned Since 2016** — We're a Bend-based small business, not a franchise. Every booking supports a local family and team.
