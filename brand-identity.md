**Status:** Demo / Mockup (fictional business)

---

## Typography

| Role | Font | Weights | Applied To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / Headings | Playfair Display | 400, 500, 600, 700 | h1–h3, hero title, section headings |
| Body / UI | DM Sans | 400, 500, 600 | Paragraphs, nav labels, buttons, form fields |

Both available on Google Fonts.

**Rules:**
- Playfair Display supports all standard weights (400–700) — no single-weight restriction
- Hero title: Playfair Display 700, clamp(2rem, 5vw, 3.5rem)
- Section headings: Playfair Display 700, clamp(1.5rem, 3vw, 2.2rem)
- Body: DM Sans 400, 1rem / 1.7 line-height
- Labels / buttons: DM Sans 600, 0.85–0.9rem

## Tone & Personality

Calm, trustworthy, competent. Cascade Clean Co. feels like a reliable neighbor,
not a corporate chain. Copy is warm and direct — no fluff, no jargon. Homeowners
trust them because they're consistent, vetted, and local. Sentences are short.
Confidence without hype.

---

## Do Not Use

- Heavy sans-serif headings (like Impact or Bebas Neue) — wrong feel for a residential service
- Orange or red CTAs — associate with urgency/construction, not clean/home
- Dark overall theme — this is a light, airy, clean-feeling brand
- Teal below 30% opacity on white — insufficient contrast
