What We Do

House Cleaning Services in Bend, OR

From weekly upkeep to move-out deep cleans, we handle every job with the same care and consistency Bend homeowners have relied on since 2016.

Regular Cleaning

Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly visits to keep your home consistently fresh. We handle dusting, vacuuming, mopping, bathrooms, and kitchen surfaces on every visit.

Deep Cleaning

An intensive top-to-bottom clean for homes that need extra attention. Includes inside appliances, baseboards, window sills, and all the spots regular cleaning doesn't reach.
